- **Alchemical Transmutation** -{*A*}- Transmute one element into another, or sacrifice matter to create something of equal worth; with strain, transmute wounds and ailments themselves.
- **Alchemical Concoction** -{*A*}- Brew potions that cure or inflict ailments; you may attempt to disenchant what you identify.
- **Ashes to Ashes** -{*S*}- Abyss Mastery. Turn a corpse within sight into an Abyssal under your command.
- **Godsmith** -{*S*}- Abyss Smithing. Seal a recently-dead soul into an item, binding some of its power - or consciousness - for your use.
- **Crown of Horns** -{*S*}- Sever your own limb for a surge of power; dismembering a dying foe lets you seal or transform them on the spot.
