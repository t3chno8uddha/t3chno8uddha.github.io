- **Apex Predator** -{*P*}- +1♦ against an unsuspecting foe and during ambushes. A successful strike makes your next **push** free.
- **Sleeping Soldier** -{*P*}- +1♦ to parry; a clean parry feeds your counter, and turns even feints to your favour. Straining yourself before a parry guarantees Trivial Peril and Miraculous Payoff.
- **Overwhelming Odds** -{*P*}- +1♦ when outnumbered. Increased Payoff for you and your allies.
- **Item Crash** -{*A*}- +1♦ unarmed, and any new weapon strikes truer the first time; you may break a weapon to take its power into yourself for the next 12 hours.
- **Elemental Infusion** -{*A*}- Imbue a weapon or item with an element, or with sacred or profane force.
