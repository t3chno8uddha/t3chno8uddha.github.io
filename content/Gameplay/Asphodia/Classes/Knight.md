- **Weapons Master** -{*P*}- +1♦ when attacking with a weapon you hold.
- **Knight's Codex** -{*P*}- When you act to defend or stand up for an ally, both you and the ally gain +1♦.
- **Arthurian Tales** -{*A*}- Recounting a rousing tale of adventure, you and all who listen gain +1♦ on your next action; the bigger the audience, the more it stirs them.
- **Fame and Fortune** -{*A*}- Your renown precedes you; +1♦ when addressing crowds, and again whenever your allies witness your deeds.
- **History in the Making** -{*S*}- Endure a blow that would fell you, staying on your feet, or extend the same blessing to an ally. Once spent, you cannot do it again until you rest.
