- **Condemnation** -{*P*}- Your physical attacks may inflict mental ailments.
- **Arcane Conjuring** -{*A*}- Manifest Arcanum - raw magic - up to your own body's mass, shaped as you will. Manifesting more than your limit will strain you.
- **Electromagical Pulse** -{*A*}- Loose a burst of energy that disrupts magic and leaves its targets hexed.
- **Sigil Magic** -{*A*}- Inscribe a remote Sigil on any object; speak through it, or trigger it to burst with its element.
- **Grand Spellcraft** -{*S*}- Once a day, craft a spell on the spot, imbuing it with destruction, creation, the warping of space, or the bending of time. Said spell can be imbued into a scroll for repeat use.
