There are 9 elements in the game - Agni, Aqua, Flora, Terra, Ventus, Volta, Sacrum, Profanum and Arcanum.

Agni, Aqua, Flora and Sacrum are **Solar** elements.
Ventus, Volta, Terra and Profanum are **Lunar** elements. 
Arcanum is raw magic, and belongs to neither category.

Solar and Lunar elements are counterparts of each other.

| Solar  | Lunar    |
| ------ | -------- |
| Agni   | Ventus   |
| Aqua   | Volta    |
| Flora  | Terra    |
| Sacrum | Profanum |
It is worth noting that Empyreans and Abyssals (seen in class abilities) are also Solar and Lunar powers respectively, despite not technically being elements.

Certain targets may be **Weak** or **Strong** to certain elements.
In the case where a player attacks a target with its weakness, after the roll, the GM rolls another die into their pool, and then reads the highest.
However, in the case where a player attacks a target with its strength, after the roll, the GM removes the highest die from the pool, and then reads the highest. If there are no die left, or if the roll was made with disadvantage, it counts as automatic failure.

Targets may also **Null** an element, rendering all efforts with it ineffective, **Drain** an element, which lets them heal from damage, and **Repel** an element, making it bounce back to the source.

Elements each have an Apex form, which changes how they behave.

|  **Apex Title** | Description                                    | Element  |
| --------------: | ---------------------------------------------- | :------- |
|    **Infernal** | *Burns in flame that doesn't extinguish.*      | Agni     |
|     **Glacial** | *Freezes in ice that doesn't melt.*            | Aqua     |
|      **Faunal** | *The bending of souls and wills.*              | Flora    |
|   **Celestial** | *Grants spectral movement.*                    | Ventus   |
| **Thundergale** | *Grants lightning-fast movement.*              | Volta    |
| **Centripetal** | *The bending of forces of gravity.*            | Terra    |
|     **Radiant** | *Radiant wounds on vile targets never heal.*   | Sacrum   |
| **Blasphemous** | *Blasphemous wounds grow larger on their own.* | Profanum |
|   **Lifeblood** | *Cannot be resisted or blocked.*               | Arcanum  |
