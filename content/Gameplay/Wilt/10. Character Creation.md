Upon creating a character, pick the name, the archetype and the heritage.

Then, mark your archetype's and heritage's Act ranks. Repeat act ranks add up.

Then, pick any ability from the wheel for free, and fill its ♥ on the sheet.

Finally, you have two points to unlock an ability, raise an Act's rank, or one of each.
