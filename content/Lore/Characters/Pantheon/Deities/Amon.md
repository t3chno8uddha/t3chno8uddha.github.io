An Occult being, and one of the earliest recorded Abyssals in history. A vile cannibal, one of the few Abyssals known to eat its own kind.

Abyssals sick of his reign banded together to cast a Radiant spell - one that would harm many of them - to expunge him entirely. 

However, since the sacred spell was cast by profane mouths, it ended up erasing only Amon's body, keeping his Idolon alive in the Fleeting Plane, where it latched onto the first body it saw, that of [[Morgan]].
