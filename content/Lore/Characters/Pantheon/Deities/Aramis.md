A colossal dragon hovering over [[Kronitia]].

>[!spoilers]
> [[Cronika]]'s Empyrean, manifested in place of her corpse after her battle with [[Draca]]. despite being a Dragon - and therefore, Draca's child - the mother rejected the beast, leaving it to retreat to Kronitia.
> 
>There, it met [[Rafael]], right after he had Execrated his brother. Rafael tamed the beast with mutual grief and love, and Aramis swiftly became loyal to him.
>
>In 1441, Aramis guards the ruins of the empire, at Rafael's beck and call.
