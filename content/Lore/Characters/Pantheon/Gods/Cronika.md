Goddess of **discipline** and **time**.

Governs over **[[Wonderlay]]**, alongside [[Lancast]] and [[Fidico]], as well as **[[Kronitia]]**.

Her most noteworthy relics are the **Tomes of Past, Present and Future**, which depict exactly what they say they depict.

>[!spoilers]
>Deceived by the political intrigue incited by the [[Second Son]], she attacked [[Draca]] in 1400, and perished by her hand.
