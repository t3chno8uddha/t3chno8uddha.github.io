God of **craftsmanship**, **solitude** and the patron god of **[[Aél]]**.

Governs over **[[Ehelden]]** and **[[Infernait]]**.

His most noteworthy relic is the **Hamaul**, a Centripetal hammer that unmakes all it touches, and rebuilds it anew.
