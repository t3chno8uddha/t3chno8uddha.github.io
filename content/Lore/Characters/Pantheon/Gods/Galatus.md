God of **camaraderie**, **hope**, and the patron god of **[[Gail]]**.

Governs over **[[Galatea]]**.

His most noteworthy relic is the **Kaleidoscope of Galatus**, a looking glass that lets the user find any soul they want.

 Sometime around the 10th century, Galatus went into slumber in what has since come known as his tomb. In 1413, His sleeping body was infected with [[Drakenblight]] and defeated by the caravan. He is currently believed to be dead.

>[!spoilers]
>His infected body remained dead for 10 years before entombing itself back and starting a healing process. Galatus is in fact still alive, and resting in the mountains of Ga'Al-Tey.
