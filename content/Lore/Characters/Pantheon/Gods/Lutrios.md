Tree god of **flora** and **fauna**.

Governs over **[[Papillopolis]]**, the **[[Misthrong Holt]]**, and all nature, alongside [[Mateo]].

His most noteworthy relics are the **Leaves of Life**, which can create life where it doesn't exist.
