Goddess of **survival**, **adaptation**, and the patron god of **[[Lazi]]**.

Governs over **[[The Arena]]** and the **[[Lazalan Atoll]]**.

Her most noteworthy relic is the **Remnant's Mesh**, a chain-mail armor that can endure any element.
