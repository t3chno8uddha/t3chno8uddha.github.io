God of **blood**, **night**, and the patron god of **[[Vampyr]]**.

Governs over the shadows. 

His most noteworthy relic is the **New Moon Amulet**, a Blasphemous pendant that lets the bearer travel in and out of Te'Al and the Umbral Plane at will.
