A guru residing in [[Rubimel]], able to guide visitors from the swamp town into [[Papillopolis]] - The First Forest.

Guru Dizang posesses a singular power - being both a zealot and an occultist, he completely transcends the Solar and Lunar split.

He's famed for using his power to imprison Idolons inside relics - not as punishment, but as preservation for lost souls and those who need an escape.

In that sense, he is a walking prison with his inmates being trapped into small totems, often voluntarily - to escape from capital punishment, until statute of limitations has passed, for example.

>[!spoilers]
>Witnessing the fire of Papillopolis in 1413, and hearing the truth of the [[Second Son]]'s designs from the [[Shrieking Titan]], he had since dedicated himself to a greater purpose - of Abyss Smithing all of mankind and destroying the conduit, thus directly sending every Idolon into the Sempiternal Plane, where they can exist free of worldy dangers.
>
>The very intent of this had made him the direct enemy of all twelve gods.
