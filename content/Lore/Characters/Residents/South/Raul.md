A chronopath of the Holy Kronitian Empire - and its prince, a direct descendant of [[Cronika]] and [[Galatus]]' daughter [[Diya]], and beyond Death's reach in 1400. Raul can harness [[Chronokinesis]]. 

After Cronika's death, Raul wanted to get to the bottom of it all first. He fought his brother [[Rafael]], lost, and had his body - alongside his powers and memories - sliced up and thrown all around the world, forcing him to rebuild himself extremity to extremity. With the help of a mysterious benefactor, he got his head and some of his limbs back; an army of small demons substitutes for muscles everywhere else. His memories and powers return with his limbs, and he believes some are scattered through time - which is why he sought [[Temeran]]'s amulet, that lets people travel through shadows, to instead travel through time.

In 1413, he joined the party at [[Eethert Plains]] and travelled with them until Rafael, tracing him to the destruction of [[Mansfield]], attacked him at sea, defeated him again, and trapped his soul, hiding it away where no one would find it. As his last act, Raul preserved the lives of his allies, leaving them frozen in time, in the ocean, for 28 years.

In 1441, Raul is trying to kill his brother to prevent the world war, having seen the tragedy it made happen and felt the tragedy of his own Empire - though how, with his soul still hidden, goes unrecorded.
