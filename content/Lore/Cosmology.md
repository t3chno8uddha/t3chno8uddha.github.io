### Genesis:
In the beginning, there was nothing but [[Asphea]]. Asphea grew lonely, and she created herself a husband - matter. Came the Fleeting Plane - a world for all that lives, and then the earth - Asphodia - the sun, the moon, and together, they bore twelve children, the gods:

**[[Adamaal]]**, the Resolute. God of war and hunting, and the patron god of [[Undine]].
**[[Lancast]]**, the Chivalrous. God of honour and righteousness.
**[[Cronika]]**, the Merciless. Goddess of discipline and time.
**[[Fidico]]**, the Grateful. God of faith and modesty.
**[[Lutrios]]**, the Radiant. Tree god of flora and fauna.
**[[Magna]]**, the Elegant. Goddess of magic and entertainment.
**[[Crucito]]**, the Prolific. God of craftsmanship, solitude, and the patron god of [[Aél]].
**[[Draca]]**, the Covetous. Goddess of motherhood, destruction and the patron god of Dragons.
**[[Temeran]]**, the Duplicitous. God of blood, night, and the patron god of [[Vampyr]].
**[[Abara]]**, the Manifold. Goddess of love, deceit, and the patron god of [[Fae]].
**[[Maluma]]**, the Stalwart. Goddess of survival, adaptation, and the patron god of [[Lazi]].
and **[[Galatus]]**, the Amiable. God of camaraderie, hope, and the patron god of [[Gail]].

After being put on the world, the gods made battle with each other, to see how they would split the territories. Four of the gods abstained from the war - Lancast, Fidico, Abara and Lutrios. Lutrios instead rooted himself on an island, and created the first forest, from which came trees, plants and birthed flora. The gods Lancast and Fidico instead used their resources to incite their siblings to more understanding and compassionate ends. Abara did not fight, but instead she used her magic to turn her siblings against each other, not out of spite but because it is her nature.
Lancast had intervened in the battle only once, to stop Adamaal from slaying Temeran. Fidico, meanwhile, only stepped in to save humans and other beings caught in the collateral damage.

As the battle ended, Galatus won the most territory, and it was named the Great Lands of [[Galatea]].
Adamaal won second most. He only had space for two kingdoms, but the ocean was entirely his domain.
Cronika won an island, where she made her empire [[Kronitia]], and a kingdom she shared with Fidico and Lancast - [[Wonderlay]].
Draca won two mountain ranges. On one she built her home, [[Drakengard]], and another - [[Dhidalah]] - she gave to her and Temeran's son - Daedalus, the first Vampyr.
Maluma won an archipelago titled the [[Lazalan Atoll]]. Harsh living conditions, but the Lazi fit there just fine. She also kept the battlefield on which the God War was waged, called Asphodel's Arena.
Magna won the southern half of the northern continent, and used her magic to build a castle kingdom in her name.
Crucito won an island, as well the frozen northern half of the northern continent. He gave it to his and Abara's children - Aél to be used as home.
Abara threw the war, only claiming two small islands; and Temeran lost the war entirely. It is said that his territory is everywhere where the sun doesn't fall.

There is another god, a human called [[Mateo]], one of the most prolific ancient hunters, left his home in [[Adamail]]. He traveled the world, in pursuit of finding himself and discovering all it had to offer, and became the first Oracle - Mateo, the Ardent.

Once something dies, it is returned to its primordial form as an Idolon - a soul, in other words - and different gods have different afterlives, some which they share. If it was either meant to be, or the god allows the Idolon entry, and the Idolon is free to do however it wishes. Sometimes, the sibling gods do trial, to decide which of them has the right to the Idolon.

If no god lets a soul entry, it has no choice but to roam the Sempiternal Plane as an Idolon. Whether the gods allow it in or not can change, based on the gods' whims. 
Idolons manifest into the Fleeting Plane in various ways, such as Abyssals or Empyreans.

 While the Idolon is the raw energy that makes up a soul, Empyreans and Abyssals are the different filters through which a soul is funneled. Empyreans are the manifestations of what a soul believes to be its best, most virtuous qualities; meanwhile Abyssals are the traits and qualities a soul runs and hides from the most.

There are Six planes in the realm that Asphea created.
# The Sempiternal Plane
The plane in which all other planes exist. Domain of the afterlives, the gods, and their mother.
# The Fleeting Plane
The plane in which the world, its inhabitants, and everything passing resides. Idolons float in and out of the Fleeting Plane.
# The Umbral Plane
A fragment of The Sempiternal Plane, which contains reflections of all Abyssals, living or passed.
# The Solar Plane
A plane that exists inside every Idolon, separate of the rest yet connected to all other Solar Planes.
# The Elemental Plane
A plane of concentrated elemental energy that grows and changes without rhyme or reason, constantly crashing into itself.
# The Undone Plane
A plane lost in time, severed from all other. It hosts things and souls that have lost their place in time and do not belong in it anymore.
