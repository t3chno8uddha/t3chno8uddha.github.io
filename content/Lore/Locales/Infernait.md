A prison island that is one giant volcano. Staff buildings and a low-security block sit outside the cone; the dangerous prisoners are held on floors carved down the volcano's throat. The very bottom "floor" is a cluster of caged cells hung by heavy chains directly over the lava — solitary confinement.

Most of the prison's staff are [[Aél]] from the Northern Petal.

Deep in the volcano's lava, [[Infernal Arbiter]] resides. 

In 1413, **Magnus**, **Lillianele** and **Maxill** infiltrated the prison as inmates (via murdering the Karacolian prince) to break Oleander out for **[[Rafael]]**, who waited on the far shore. They also broke out [[Morgan]].
