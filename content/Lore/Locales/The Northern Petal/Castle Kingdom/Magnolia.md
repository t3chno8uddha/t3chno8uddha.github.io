The only realm in Asphodia directly presided over by a god and not a government entity. Magnolia is hailed as the grand capital of magic in Asphodia. It has no single definitive law enforcement. Instead, [[Magna]] likes to simply observe the humans living in her kingdom. The Kingdom is built atop an enormous castle-like structure. 

There's a magical force that *propels* vertically from the floors and ceilings of the castle, that prevent magic being used to harm or kill another person - however regular means aren't prevented. This is to preserve the sanctity of magic and order, and not out of concern for human life.

Different cities of the kingdom are self-governed by different organisations, which often clash over political, ideological or petty differences.  

More on [[Magnolian Factions]]...

 Crime is high in many districts of Magnolia, especially organised crime conducted by various criminal and self-enforcing organisations striving to make the castle theirs. As such, there are neutral erudite wizards called Wardens, who look into and seek out crimes committed by regular means - therefore requiring regular physical knowledge, such as science, forensics, investigative skills, so on and so forth. Despite them being a neutral faction without any territories, they are not seen as particularly welcome in any of the other factions territories, on the account of them following their own rules.

Magnolia is also considered one of the most highly intelligent kingdoms of Asphodia, despite the major amount of crime, corruption and political tensions surrounding it. They don't concern themselves with export, seeing how between their rich knowledge of both magic and science, they can maintain their entire kingdom, give or take.

There are lots of ways to entertain yourself in Magnolia, depending which part of the kingdom you're in. A lot of gambling houses, bordellos, music, and other kinds of entertainment that can be delivered to anyone using magic and primitive technology powered by said magic.

Their prime entertainment is the Idolon Arena, held four times every year, on the bridge over the Althean River - a fight to the death where champions bring out their Idolons - be it Abyssal or Empyrean - to compete in a duel. The tournament is officially sponsored by the Magnists, and they preside over it indiscriminately.
