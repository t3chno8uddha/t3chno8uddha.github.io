An island empire founded by [[Cronika]], and home to [[Raul]] and [[Rafael]] Tannengards.
Above it stands the **[[Sempiternal Observatory]]**.

After Cronika's death in 1400, the Empire, alongside its residents, was swiftly destroyed. In reality, it was moved to the Undone Plane. The ruins of the empire are still present in the Fleeting Plane, on the Southern Petal
