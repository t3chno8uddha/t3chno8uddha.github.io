The **largest kingdom in Asphodia, won by [[Galatus]] in the God War.**
Home to Graphel, as well as prince Hemos Solare and his sister Melodia.

In 1413 the witch-hunter **[[Cornelius]]** was brainwashed by [[Rafael]] to control Galatea from within, and causing political imbalance by segregating magical beings from regular humans.
