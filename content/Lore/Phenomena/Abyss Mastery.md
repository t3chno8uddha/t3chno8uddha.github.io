A heretical art of bringing the dead back to life, in their abyssal form. needs only the Idolon of the deceased and not their flesh, although the flesh helps preserve the soul for the ritual

There is hear-say of a zealous reframing of the ritual, which revives the dead not as their Abyssals, but as their Empyreans.

Abyss Mastery also has a different use, in form of Abyss Smithing. Abyss Smithing seals an Abyssal inside a vessel or conduit, which then manifests part of their essence and abilities. Abyss Smithing is often used to create powerful weapons and equipment.
