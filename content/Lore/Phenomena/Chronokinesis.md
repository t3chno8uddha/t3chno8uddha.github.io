Chronokinesis is the ability to bend the flow of time to one's whim.

Chronokinesis is not a power that can be learned, only belonging in the repertoire of  skilled Chronopaths - direct descendants of [[Cronika]] and [[Galatus]]' daughter, [[Diya]]. Such descendants of gods are often referred to as Godbloods. 

Chronopaths are souls that exist infinitely between their birth and their present, as opposed to those who only pass the fleeting plane.

The only known remaining Chronopaths are  [[Raul]] and [[Rafael]] Tannengards, and [[Graphel]] Degrie. ![[chronokinesis.png]]
