Exanimates are the undead, briefly speaking. They are passed souls possessing their own remnants. The two main kinds of Exanimates are Calxes - skeletal exanimates - and Necrophages - fleshed exanimates.

There are many ways of becoming an Exanimate - be it a revival gone wrong; decades having passed after dying whilst wearing [[Vessel Rings]]; or a stray Idolon mustering enough willpower to force itself back in its corpse - after, say,  having been locked out of afterlives due to casting execrations.
