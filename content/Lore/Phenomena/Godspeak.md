Godspeak, put simply, is the language of the gods. Only the firstborn twelve can speak it, and it has the power to manifest from mere words into corporeity.

Godspeak are deemed as the absolute power, and is how the gods shaped Asphodia to their whims. [[Edicts]] are said to have derived from a weaker manifestation of Godspeak.
