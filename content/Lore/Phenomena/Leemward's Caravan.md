[[Leemward]]'s Caravan is the name of an enchanted rucksack that unfolds into a carriage.

Leemward passed it to [[Francis]], and his band, after being freed from the Condors that sieged [[The Library of Galatea]]. The party have been known to use it since 1413 onward.

The object can compress itself and everything held inside the carriage into a bag, fit to carry on one's back or over one's shoulder.
