[[Magnolia]]'s main self-governing entities include:
# Magnists
The largest families and bloodlines of old, children of [[Magna]] that know magic both through their mind and their flesh. Despite looking down on the other factions, they do accept and allow them to live and exist outside the castle's inner ward. Their most common method of punishment is banishment and exile, but not from the castle but from the inner ward itself, out into the rest of the factions' territories. Travel in and out of the inner walls is strictly enforced, mostly by old and wizened members of the community.

**Influence:** *Magnolia's Chambertown, The Old Bailey,  [[Abara]]'s Donjon, The Inner Courtyard*
**Tensions:** *Cartogas, Incarnists* 
**Peace:** *Falconians*

# Falconians
Founders of New [[Adamail]] - old fashioned, mask-donning authoritarians. They try to enforce laws strictly and swiftly, disregarding whether or not it claims a civilian, a criminal, or one of their own. Perhaps the least corrupt but most ruthless of the factions. Their Adamailian origins often translate into their tastes, inhospitality but also a sense of pride and righteousness.

**Influence:** *New Adamail, Redflock (North Coast)*
**Tensions:** *Cartogas*
**Peace:** *Magnists, Incarnists*

# Cartogas
These cloaked wizards, a lot of whom are banished Magnists, are seen donning their black and purple coats, helping them hide in plain sight. The wizards of Cartogas are exceptionally corrupt and are rumoured to often spy on other factions, meddle in their affairs, and run majority of the organised crime in the kingdom.

**Influence:** *Greylight, Redflock (South Coast)*
**Tensions:** *Falconians, Magnists*
**Peace:** *Incarnists*

# Reasonists
Comprised initially of Magnists that have left the inner walls, mostly of their own accord. These families don't often share Magnists' purist sentiments, choosing instead to live harmoniously with the other factions. Reasonists are the only faction that does not condone and execute capital punishment, instead relying on tightly maintained prisons.

**Influence:** *Applegate*
**Tensions:** *Magnists, Cartogas*
**Peace:** *Incarnists*

# Incarnists
Making up around a fifth of the population of Magnolia are Incarnists - immigrants of otherwise regular descent who for one reason or another have relocated their entire life into the Castle Kingdom. Their rules of governance are the most primitive - often having elected representatives, simple laws and the most manual labour establishments.

**Influence:** *Long Outskirt, Althea, Falconians (West Coast)*
**Tensions:** *Magnists*
**Peace:** *Falconians, Reasonists*
