An Occultist Vampyr. Next in line for the Galatean throne, and brother to princess Melodia.
In 1413, he left his kingdom to travel with the rest of the troupe.
Due the Galatean Queen's habitual infidelity, Hemos is in fact the son of Duke Daedalus. Lucas is his son, the Duke's grandson.
