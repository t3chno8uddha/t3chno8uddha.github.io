Next in line for the throne of [[Galatea]], and brother to [[princess Melodia]].

In 1413, he left his kingdom to travel with the rest of the caravan.

Due the Galatean Queen's habitual infidelity, Hemos is in fact the son of [[Duke Daedalus]]. [[Lucas Daedalus Solare]] is his son, the Duke's grandson.
