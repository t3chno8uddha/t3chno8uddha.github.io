A man-made assassin created to sell out to the highest bidder.

Escaped [[Lanaganne]] with his mother, until their unfortunate separation. Spent the rest of his life looking for her.

Underwent a trial-vision in [[Papillopolis]] in 1413: a long-slain beast underfoot, and standing triumphant above it a silver-haired woman with Clover's eyes - the mother. The visage, elusive by nature, tried to trick Clover into letting the mother go, as a test of determination.
