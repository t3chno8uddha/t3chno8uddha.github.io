Born in [[Papillopolis]], and raised by a grandmother whose grave lies near [[Rubimel]].

After the grandmother's passing, Fey adventured alone - from Adamail to Galatea - fighting, stealing, loving and losing, and finding the scrap of a legendary book: a page of Magna's spell-book, bearing a single spell, Telelink.

In 1413, barred from entry at [[Mansfield]] and tracked by Mansfielders toward [[Rubimel]].
