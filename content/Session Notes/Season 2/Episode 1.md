### **Setting & Initial Situation**

- The characters awaken floating in the vast, open ocean with no memory of how they got there.
- A mysterious storm rages in the distance, crackling with unnatural energy.
- The water is unnervingly warm (or freezing cold), hinting at something unnatural beneath.
- A distant island, shipwreck, or floating debris offers the only visible hope for survival.
- Strange marine creatures swim below, watching them with eerie intelligence.
- A whirlpool begins forming nearby, slowly pulling them in.

### **Survival Challenges**

- **Exhaustion & Drowning:** The characters must keep themselves afloat or find a raft.
- **Hunger & Thirst:** Saltwater is undrinkable, and there’s no immediate food. What resources can they gather?
- **Predators:** Sharks, sea serpents, or massive tentacles lurking beneath the waves.
- **Weather Hazards:** Sudden storms, scorching sun, or unnatural fogs.
- **Limited Equipment:** Did they wake up with any gear? Do they scavenge anything floating nearby?

### **Exploration & Encounters**

- A shipwreck drifts nearby, full of supplies—but it’s also haunted or cursed.
- A massive, ancient sea creature rises from the deep and offers them a cryptic choice.
- A raft of castaways drifts toward them, but are they allies or desperate foes?
- A portal or magical rift opens beneath them, leading to another realm (underwater city, elemental plane, etc.).
- A Undine kingdom is at war, and the characters are caught in the middle.
- A ghostly lighthouse appears on the horizon, promising salvation but hiding dark secrets.

### **Possible Factions & NPCs**

- **Shipwreck Survivors:** Desperate, paranoid, and possibly cannibalistic.
- **Pirates or Privateers:** They might help—or see the characters as easy targets.
- **Cultists of the Deep:** Worshippers of an ancient sea god who believe the players’ arrival is prophecy.
- **A Lone Fisherman:** An eccentric hermit who has lived at sea for years, offering wisdom or nonsense.
- **Undine & Deep Dwellers:** Some want to help, others see the players as intruders or food.
- **Sentient Ocean Entity:** The ocean itself is alive and communicating in strange ways.

### **Plot Twists & Story Hooks**

- The characters were sacrificed to the sea by an unknown enemy and must find out why.
- The ocean is actually an illusion, a prison designed to test or torment them.
- They were sailors or adventurers before, but something wiped their memories.
- The water is slowly rising—land is vanishing, and they must find a way to stop it.
- The ocean is a battlefield between gods or ancient forces, and the players are caught in the middle.
- Something massive beneath the waves is waking up, and only the players can stop it—or help it rise.
