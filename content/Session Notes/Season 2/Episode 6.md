The players are exploring Magnolia, when someone runs into the district yelling and enlisting help on the academy's behalf. The players will be forewarned to volunteer the help for this specific session.

[[Brejur Academy]]

When they arrive at the Brejur academy, they find that the place is completely normal looking and free of harm - except something is clearly wrong, initially depicted similar to a mass hysteria. The people are just acting... strange.  Almost as if they're following some kind of template. for behaviors.

They welcome the party to stay, and charge what they describe to be a comically large amount of money - 10 gold. They may not learn anything worthwhile by listening to the NPCs, but they're welcome to peruse the academy grounds to find scrolls of their own, as well as interact with its magical capabilities.

They may find another **arch-library** - entered through a small wardrobe in a regular library. It opens up into the same hall that it opened up to in Rubimel. The players may also learn that this is a new fast-travel method, and commune with the unaffected scholars in the library, which is really a pocket dimension that certain populations secretly have access to.

In the library, they may find new spells, or read more about the gods - including their relics.

At night, a group of Abyssals attack, but they only attack the PCs, and the magical residents of the academy do very little to fight them off.

Trying to take on the kid in a fight results in him turning the entire academy's population into the Manipede, a massive magical human worm.
