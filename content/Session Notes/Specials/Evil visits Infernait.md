The sun showers you in its warmth, beckoning you to continue your pilgrimage, not that you recall where it might lead you. You have only your  dragon-bone wand on you, as well as some coin, and a debilitating hunger. What do you do?"

"Kicked out from yet another town, your travels have brought you farther from your home than ever. There are some prestigious towns above you, that much you know. The south you had sailed from doesn't have much to offer you anymore. You've made a friend, arguably even two, but not even they would proudly announce associating with the likes of you. You're not short on cash, yet, but your luck can't last for that much longer now. What do you do?"
![[Amethist_coast.png]]
Magnus, after washing up on shore, learns about a city mysteriously sharing his namesake. In a carriage heading to it he is met with Lilli, and get greeted by a cloaked figure who introduces himself as Rafael. Raf promises a hefty sum in exchange for breaking two inmates out of the prison island - Infernait. Proposes they go in as inmates, locate the two in need of freeing and do what's necessary to get them out. Raf promises to be waiting on the other end of the shore to sail them out. First order of business was becoming an international criminal, which is easy, given that they're travelling alongside the young prince of Karacolia, who takes leisurely strolls away from his province in order to see the world. Killing him right here and now would secure everything they could need in order to get thrown into Infernait, considering the Karacol Province doesn't believe in capital punishment. Rafael also entrusts them with a bag - filled with clothes and bones, inscribed in golden on blue - Maxill.
![[Asphodel Field2.png]]
Infernait as a prison takes up its entire island. There are several buildings outside of the volcano reserved for staff, as well as one building for the less dangerous kind of prisoners - of which there's 14 total. The rest of the prison is spanning the spine of the titular volcano, Infernait.
There are floors carved around in the volcano's gaping mouth, and those floors guard different inmates. The very bottom floor is not even a floor - it's enclosed cages, hanging by heavy chains right above the surface of the lava. That's the solitary confinement sector, and it's also where they have to break one of the prisoners out - one Oleander Gaust. Oleander can turn into a poisonous mist and need only punch an unarmoured target to kill them, instantly turning their bones into equipment.
![[Inside_Infernait.png]]
The rest of the session has the players break out of the prison with Oleander Gaust. They may choose whatever path they want. The session will lead them into phantom saltwaters in which they may continue their quest.
